## Brand & Style
The design system establishes a premium, eco-luxury herbal skincare presence that bridges ancient botanical wellness with clinical purity. It evokes tranquility, purity, sensorial indulgence, and uncompromised organic integrity. 

The aesthetic is **Organic Modern Luxury**: clean editorial whitespace, tactile earthen surfaces, grounded forest greens, and subtle metallic warmth. Surfaces avoid sterile clinical coldness by utilizing warm linen-tinted whites, creamy sand underlays, and soft botanical greens that honor Sri Lankan herbal heritage while commanding an elevated direct-to-consumer e-commerce experience.

## Colors
The palette balances deep chlorophyll tones with soft earth pigments and restrained artisanal accents.

- **Primary (`#1B4332`)**: Deep Forest Green serves as the primary anchor for headers, primary CTA buttons, luxury badges, and key high-contrast elements.
- **Secondary (`#52B788`)**: Botanical Sage brings fresh life to hover states, secondary highlights, tags, and supportive accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C5A059`)**: Muted Warm Gold is strictly reserved for luxury certifications, star ratings, premium highlights, and award crests.
- **Neutral (`#1E2923`)**: Charcoal Forest Slate replaces harsh synthetic blacks for all body copy and primary typography, maintaining softness and high legibility.
- **Canvas & Backdrops**: Default canvas utilizes `surface-warm` (`#FCFBF7`), while card containers, modules, and segmented sections rest on `surface-sand` (`#F7F4EE`) or `botanical-tint` (`#EBF1EE`).
- **Sale & Urgency (`#CE2828`)**: Used sparingly for promotional price strikethroughs, low stock counters, and limited botanical drop tags.

## Typography
The typographic pairing unifies editorial herbal prestige with contemporary direct-to-consumer clarity.

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles**: Set in **Playfair Display**. Features subtle italic flourishes for botanical descriptions, ritual instructions, and natural ingredient callouts.
- **Product Titles, UI, & Body Text**: Set in **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Delivers supreme legibility across dense ingredient listings, clinical testing proofs, customer reviews, and transactional cart flows.
- **Micro-Copy & Category Overlines**: Rendered in `label-caps` with uppercase tracking (+0.1em) to frame luxury skincare collections cleanly.
- **Pricing**: Clear, tabular tabular figures in Plus Jakarta Sans ensure the Sri Lankan Rupee (`LKR`) denomination reads with immediate clarity.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive 12-column fluid grid system across desktop and tablet viewports, adapting down to a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints**: Desktop at `1200px+`, Tablet at `768px - 1199px`, Mobile below `768px`.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Generous vertical breathing room distinguishes product collections, active herbal stories, and reviews. Sections are padded using `space-xl` scaled dynamically (up to `5rem` on desktop showcase panels).
- **Margins & Gutters**: Outer canvas margins use `3rem` on desktop for an airy, boutique storefront feel, contracting to `1.25rem` on mobile to optimize screen real estate.
- **Grid Density**: E-commerce catalog views utilize 4-column cards on desktop, 2-column cards on tablet, and 2-column asymmetric or single featured stacks on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through warm tonal layering and soft, diffused botanical-tinted drop shadows rather than synthetic gray blurs.

- **Tonal Layering**: Containers elevate primarily through surface contrasts. Baseline elements sit on `surface-warm` (`#FCFBF7`), card modules nest upon `surface-sand` (`#F7F4EE`), and active modals or sticky navbars lift on pure `#FFFFFF`.
- **Soft Botanical Ambient Shadows**: Floating cards, dropdown menus, and sticky cart panels apply low-opacity shadows tinted with deep forest slate (`rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.06)` to `rgba(27, 67, 50, 0.12)`) with a blur spread of 16px to 32px.
- **Ghost Borders**: Crisp, delicate borders using `rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.08)` or `rgba(197, 160, 89, 0.25)` provide clean containment for product thumbnails and ingredient breakdowns without heavy visual weight.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy champions organic curvature reminiscent of botanical petals, polished river stones, and smooth earthen jars.

- **Base Radius**: Components use `0.5rem` (8px) for buttons, text inputs, and badges.
- **Card Containers (`rounded-lg`)**: Product cards, review modules, and promotional banners adopt `1rem` (16px) corners to feel welcoming and tactile.
- **Hero Framing & Modals (`rounded-xl`)**: Primary spotlight cards and seasonal campaign panels feature `1.5rem` (24px) corners.
- **Pill Accents**: Star rating badges, benefit tags ("100% Herbal", "Cruelty-Free"), and quantity selectors adopt full pill shapes (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Solid `#1B4332` background with `#FCFBF7` text. Smooth transition to `#2D6A4F` on hover. Padded with `0.875rem 1.75rem`, rounded-md, with subtle elevation.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button featuring a 1.5px outline of `#1B4332`, transparent background, and `#1B4332` text. Hovers transition to `botanical-tint` (`#EBF1EE`).
- **Tertiary / Link**: Underlined text in `#1B4332` with an animated baseline that shifts to `#C5A059` on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Encased in `surface-sand` with ghost borders (`rgba(30, 41, 35, 0.06)`). Includes a 1:1 or 4:5 organic product render with rounded image containers.
- **Pricing Row**: Formatted with clear Sri Lankan Rupee pricing (`LKR 3,850.00`) in `price-lg`, alongside optional struck-out regular pricing in `slate-muted`.
- **Quick-Add CTA**: Floating pill button or clean bottom-anchored bar styled with primary deep forest green.

### Badges & Trust Seals
- **Botanical Badges**: Pill-shaped badges using `botanical-tint` background with `#1B4332` uppercase text (`label-caps`).
- **Star Rating Badges**: Subtle gold star cluster (`#D4AF37`) paired with a numeric score (`4.9/5`) and review count enclosed in a delicate sand capsule.
- **Purity Badges**: Circular seals highlighting certifications ("Ayurvedic Formula", "Paraben Free", "Sustainably Sourced") accented with fine `#C5A059` hairpins.

### Inputs & Quantity Pickers
- **Text Inputs**: Crisp warm white background (`#FCFBF7`) with a 1px border of `#EDE6DB`. Focus states shift to `#52B788` with a soft 2px ambient focus ring.
- **Quantity Selector**: Rounded pill container with `#1E2923` iconography, separated by gentle internal dividing lines.

### Review & Testimonial Cards
- Offset cards featuring customer verified-purchase tags, star ratings, skin concern identifiers (e.g., "Combination / Sensitive"), and italicized testimonial quotes set in Playfair Display.
